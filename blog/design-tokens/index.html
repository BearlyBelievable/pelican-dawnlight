Design tokens

By Dawnlight · Published July 12, 2026

Colors and the border radius are CSS variables at the top of main.css. You can override them in a stylesheet loaded after the theme.

1
2
3
4
:root {
    --accent-light: #2a7de1;
    --radius: 12px;
}
